**Netstall 不收集、不存储、不上传任何个人数据。**

- 没有账号，没有登录，没有分析统计，没有广告，没有第三方 SDK。
- 测量记录、测试结果和自定义目标只保存在你设备上 app 自己的文档目录里（可在「文件」app 中查看），删除 app 即全部删除。
- 进行测试时，app 会向你选择的目标服务器（苹果、Cloudflare、Netflix、哔哩哔哩等公开地址，或你自己填写的地址）发起下载与连接请求。这些服务器会像对待任何访问者一样看到你的 IP 地址；Netstall 本身不会把任何信息发送给开发者或其他任何人。
- 通过数据线由电脑驱动的自动测试，只在本机文件系统内交换文件。

**Netstall collects, stores and transmits no personal data.**

- No account, no sign-in, no analytics, no ads, no third-party SDKs.
- Measurements, test results and custom targets are kept only in the app's own documents folder on your device (visible in the Files app) and are deleted with the app.
- During a test the app downloads from and connects to the servers you pick (public endpoints such as Apple, Cloudflare, Netflix, Bilibili, or an address you enter). Those servers see your IP address as they would for any visitor; Netstall itself sends nothing to the developer or anyone else.
- Computer-driven automation over a cable exchanges files on the local file system only.
