最后更新：2026-10-03

**Netstall 不提供向开发者上传测量记录的功能。测试会连接用户所选的第三方服务器。**

- 没有账号，没有登录，没有分析统计，没有广告，没有第三方 SDK。
- 测量记录和测试结果保存在本机 app 的文档目录（可在「文件」app 中查看）；自定义目标和设置保存在本机偏好中。系统备份与用户主动分享由系统及用户控制。
- 进行测试时，app 会向你选择的目标服务器（苹果、Cloudflare、Netflix、哔哩哔哩等公开地址，或你自己填写的地址）发起下载、上传测试数据与连接请求。这些服务器会收到你的 IP 地址与请求信息；其日志留存和数据处理由各服务的隐私政策决定，不能保证服务器不留存。本 App 未核实所有端点的具体日志保留期限。
- 「复制」按钮会把测试报告写入系统剪贴板，只在你点击时发生（开启了 iCloud 通用剪贴板的设备之间会同步剪贴板，这是系统行为）。
- 仅 Debug 开发构建支持通过数据线由电脑驱动的自动测试，App Store / Release 构建不包含该入口。开发自动化只在本机文件系统内交换文件；其中的 DNS 对比步骤会向公共解析器（如 1.1.1.1、8.8.8.8）查询域名。

**Netstall has no feature for uploading measurement records to the developer. Tests connect to third-party servers selected by the user.**

- No account, no sign-in, no analytics, no ads, no third-party SDKs.
- Measurements and results are stored in the app's local Documents folder (visible in Files). Custom targets and settings are stored in local preferences. System backups and user-initiated sharing are controlled by the system and user.
- During a test the app downloads from, sends upload-test data to, and connects to the servers you pick (public endpoints such as Apple, Cloudflare, Netflix, Bilibili, or an address you enter). Those servers receive your IP address and request information. Their retention and processing are governed by their own privacy policies; no claim is made that those servers retain nothing. The app has not verified specific log-retention periods for every endpoint.
- The Copy button writes the report to the system clipboard only when you tap it (iCloud Universal Clipboard may sync it between your own devices; that is a system feature).
- Computer-driven automation is available only in Debug development builds, not App Store / Release builds. It exchanges files on the local file system only; its DNS comparison steps query public resolvers (e.g. 1.1.1.1, 8.8.8.8).
