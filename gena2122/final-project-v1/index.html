What connects these six objects?

Concentrating work in a factory can depend on a wider division of work between places. These six cases compare how materials, transport and business organization sustained that relationship. They are selected historical windows, not a sample of all American manufacturing.

    Different places, different responsibilities

    Across the six cases, final assembly is only one part of the geography. The comparison concerns changing forms of coordination; it does not establish an inevitable progression from local to global. The RCA map shows selected factory roles, while the iPhone map also includes supplier offices. Their apparent spread is not a like-for-like measure.
