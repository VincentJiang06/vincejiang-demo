> Using the process data, queue configuration, and scheduling rules below, complete the Scheduling Result Table
> on the next page. For every CPU execution interval, record its time slot, process ID, process arrival time, and
> remaining execution time at the end of that interval. A few entries are provided as checks.
> 
> 1. MLFQ Scheduling Rules
> Rule 1. Priority: If Priority(A) > Priority(B), process A is selected before B.
> Rule 2. Round Robin: Processes in the same priority queue are scheduled in round-robin order using that
> queue’s time slice (quantum).
> Rule 3. Arrivals: A new process joins the tail of the highest-priority queue. If several processes arrive at the
> same time, enqueue them in increasing process-ID (PID) order.
> Rule 4. Demotion: Once a process has consumed its full time allotment at a priority level, it moves down one
> queue and is appended to the tail of the destination queue. CPU usage at a level accumulates across time
> slices; leaving the CPU does not reset this usage.
> Rule 5. Periodic priority boost: At times S, 2S, 3S, . . ., immediately move all unfinished processes to the
> highest-priority queue and sort them by decreasing PID (largest PID runs first). A boost interrupts any
> running process and resets the per-level CPU allotment counters and time slices.
>
> 3. Corner Case Handling
> a. Simultaneous events: At the same time t, apply events in this order: (1) finish/re-enqueue/demote the
> old process when its slice ends; (2) enqueue newly arriving processes (in increasing PID order); (3) perform a
> periodic boost, if due; and (4) choose the next process if rescheduling is required.
> b. Arrivals do not interrupt a slice: If a new process arrives while another is executing, the running process
> continues until its time slice ends or it finishes. A periodic boost is the exception: it immediately interrupts
> the running process.
> c. Queue order: When a process uses a full time slice but has not exhausted its allotment, it returns to the tail
> of its current queue. If its allotment is exhausted, it joins the tail of the next lower queue. In a tie with a new
> arrival, the old process is re-enqueued first.
> d. Boost ties: Processes arriving exactly at a boost time are included in that boost’s decreasing-PID ordering,
> together with all other unfinished processes.
> e. Lowest queue: Q1 has an unlimited time allotment: processes cannot be demoted below Q1. They continue
> to use its time slice in round-robin order.
> f. Workload model: Processes do not voluntarily yield or block for I/O. A process remains runnable after
> arrival until it has consumed its specified execution time. It may stop running because its time slice expires, it
> finishes, or a periodic boost occurs.

## 先分清三个量

Time slice 是每次最多连续运行多久；Allotment 是在一个优先级累计最多运行多久。因此，Q3 里通常用满三次 10 才降级，Q2 里用满两次 40 才降级。离开 CPU 后，当前级别已经用掉的时间不会消失；boost 才会将它清零。

Remaining time 是该进程尚未完成的总 CPU 工作量，绝不是它还剩多少时间片或 allotment。每行的算法是“该进程上一回的 remaining − 本行区间长度”。Arrival time 则永远抄原题给出的首次到达时刻；降级和 boost 都不改变它。

区间使用起点包含、终点不包含的理解。例如 70–110 用掉 40 个时间单位。在时刻 110 处理结束和入队事件，再决定谁接着执行。0–10 没有进程，CPU 空闲；题目第 1 行从 10 开始，不额外填一行空闲。

## 最容易改错的四个位置

1. **70–110 不在 80 或 90 截断。** PID 57 已进入 Q2，获得 40 的时间片。PID 444 在 80 到达、333 在 90 到达，都先排入 Q3 等待。57 到 110 才让出 CPU；此时先运行 Q3 的 444。
2. **170–210 不在 175 截断。** PID 143 的 Q2 时间片继续跑满 40；175 到达的 233 要等到 210。之后 Q3 只有 233，它连续获得三个独立的 10 单位时间片，所以必须写三行 210–220、220–230、230–240。
3. **400 的 boost 覆盖之前的排队顺序。** 143 恰好在 400 用满第二次 Q2 时间片，先按规则降到 Q1；然后所有未完成进程升到 Q3，顺序为 444、333、233、143、57。这里 boost 与时间片结束恰好重合，并没有截短 360–400 这一片。各级使用量和时间片清零，但剩余工作量不变。
4. **440 到达的 366 加在 Q3 队尾。** 143 在 440 完成，此时已有 57、444、233 等待，所以顺序变成 57、444、233、366。366 不能仅因为 PID 较大就插到队首：PID 降序排序只发生在 boost，本题下次 boost 是 800，所有进程此前已完成。
